📜 Rules

How EarnLK works

  • Register free with a nickname and password, then log in.
  • Do a task and send your work. Isuru checks every submission.
  • Approved work pays into your balance — photos and short videos Rs 10–20, stories Rs 15, cooking videos Rs 500, vlogs Rs 1,000. Watching a full film pays Rs 2, maximum 5 films a day.
  • Finish 5 approved tasks and claim a Rs 50 welcome payout.
  • Normal payouts need at least Rs 100 in your balance. Isuru pays by eZ Cash, bank transfer or reload, usually within 2 days.
  • Training packages are optional courses and toolkits. Buying one never blocks free tasks. Payouts are paid from advertising revenue only — never from other members' package money.

What gets you banned

  • Fake, copied or stolen work.
  • Lying about a payment or a payout.
  • Asking other members for money.

Banned accounts lose their balance. If you think a rejection was a mistake, contact Isuru.

🔒 Privacy Policy

What we collect

Your nickname, your task submissions, your balance and payout details, and your package purchases. We do not ask for your real name, ID number or home address.

How we use it

Only to run EarnLK: check your work, pay you, and stop cheating. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash — nobody, including Isuru, can read it.

Advertising

EarnLK is funded by advertising. In future we may show ads from Google AdSense. Ad providers may use cookies to show relevant ads. You can turn off personalised ads in your Google settings.

Your data

Ask Isuru any time to delete your account and your data. Payout records are kept only as long as needed for payment records.

Contact: Isuru — through the EarnLK site.
